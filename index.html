One app for the whole transformation.

Nutrition, training and running in one place, pulling in the data people already collect on their watches, scales and favourite apps. Built for coaches first, then everyone, with a single Shift Score that says whether the transformation is on track.

This page covers the integrations, the scales, the score and the streak design, with a phased plan for building them.

The phone's health store is the universal adapter.

Nearly every wearable, scale and fitness app already writes to Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. Reading from those two stores gives Shift Garmin, Apple Watch, Samsung, Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Renpho scales, MyFitnessPal, Runna and Ladder data on day one, with no partner approvals and no aggregator fees. Direct connections come second, where they add richer data or two-way sync.

Strava is a share target, not a data source

Strava's agreement only lets a connected app show activity data to that same user, and since June 2026 bans any AI use of its data, down to loading it into a model's context window. A coach dashboard or AI coach built on Strava reads would breach both. Pull runs from the watch brand instead, and offer a one-way post to Strava.

Garmin's developer programme is paused for new applicants

Reports from September 2026 say Garmin has temporarily stopped reviewing new API requests. Garmin users are covered on day one because Garmin Connect writes to both health stores. Apply as a company the moment the programme reopens. The Training API, which pushes workouts to the watch, is the long-term prize.

Oura wants a word before you charge for Oura features

Oura's agreement effective 8 June 2026 requires written consent to charge users for API-related functionality, limits unapproved apps to ten users, and forbids aggregators from passing Oura data to AI. Integrate Oura directly, keep it out of paid tier marketing until Oura signs off, and note that newer rings need an active membership.

Fitbit timing works in your favour

The legacy Fitbit Web API switches off on 30 October 2026. Shift would start straight on the Google Health API, which has webhooks, intraday data and a reconciled multi-source stream. There is no migration debt.

Recommend three scales, connect all of them, earn a little on each.

What affiliate links actually pay

Amazon's UK programme pays low single-digit rates on health and sports products, with third-party tables putting it at roughly 1 to 3% and a 24-hour cookie, so a £25 Renpho earns well under a pound. Treat Amazon links as an onboarding convenience, not a revenue line. Better margin comes from the brands' own programmes (Withings, Renpho and Eufy all run them) and from a coach-sold starter kit where the coach marks up the scale. Check the live rate card in the Associates dashboard before quoting any number to investors.

Recommendations that fit the goal

Recommend by plan, not by catalogue: a scale and a protein source for fat loss; a scale, creatine and a lifting belt for muscle gain; a chest strap or watch for marathon and HYROX. Every recommendation should say which measure it feeds. Keep it to one or two items per goal, shown at onboarding and at milestones, so it reads as coaching rather than a shop. UK advertising rules require clear paid-link labelling.

One number that answers: am I following my plan?

The score measures how closely someone is following the plan their coach set, weighted for their goal. Results such as weight, pace and volume are tracked separately, so "doing the work but results lagging" and "results holding but habits slipping" get different responses. The score is the weighted average of how well each measure was followed. Weights are starting points per goal, and coaches can swap measures and reweight them. Missing data lowers confidence, never the score.

What makes the score trustworthy

  • It rewards following the plan, not extremes. Hitting 140 g of protein scores the same as 160 g. Under-eating never scores higher.
  • It separates "not logged" from "not done". A missing sync lowers a separate confidence figure.
  • It explains itself. Every score shows its five parts and the one action that would move it most.
  • It fits the goal. A marathon score weights long-run progression. Fat loss weights calorie adherence.
  • It is wellness, not medical. No diagnostic claims. Red flags such as rapid loss or very low intake go to the coach.

Reward showing up, forgive a bad day, never nag.

Fitness apps keep roughly 4 to 10% of users by day 30. Streaks work because losing one hurts more than earning one feels good, but a 2019 study of 1,188 fitness-app users found that piling on badges causes burnout and abandonment. Shift's loop is deliberately small.

The daily check-in

One tap, under 30 seconds: confirm today's three priorities, rate energy out of five, add an optional photo or note. Logging food or a workout anywhere that syncs in also counts. The streak is for checking in, not for being perfect.

A 13-day streak with one earned freeze used on Saturday. Today is still open. A freeze is earned every seven days in a row and you can hold two.

Why it is built this way

  • Loss aversion with a safety net. Freezes and a 24-hour repair window keep the streak motivating without turning into guilt.
  • Rewards tied to the goal. Milestones are "first 5 kg", "first 20 km week" and "four weeks above 90", not generic badges.
  • The coach sees it. A broken streak is an early warning on the coach dashboard, days before the weekly check-in.
  • Leaderboards rank effort, not weight. Coach groups can compare scores and streaks. Body measurements stay private.
  • Quiet by default. One reminder a day at a time the user picks. After two ignored days it stops and the coach gets a nudge instead.

  • Read Apple Health and Health Connect: steps, workouts, heart rate, sleep, weight, body fat, nutrition

  • Aggregators only if a long tail of devices ever blocks sales

Shift becomes the source of truth, not just the reader.

Six things to get in writing before the coach launch.

Garmin timeline

Email the Garmin Connect Developer Program with company details now so Shift is queued when applications reopen. Ask about the reported production fee.

Oura commercial consent

Written consent to include Oura-connected features in paid tiers, and approval beyond ten users, before any marketing mentions Oura.

WHOOP's position on inference

The terms ban model training but say nothing about inference. Confirm with WHOOP API support that an AI coach summarising a member's data is allowed.

Apple and Google review

Health data may only be used to improve health management, with permission. The privacy policy and consent screens must say that the coach and the AI coach see it.

Affiliate rates and labelling

Pull the live Amazon UK rate card and compare the Withings, Renpho and Eufy programmes. All links need paid-link labelling in the UK.

UK GDPR and health data

Body and activity data is special-category data. A data protection impact assessment, explicit consent and a data-sharing agreement with each coach are needed before the pilot.
